How to Command the Oars

1. The Four Lanes & Hit Line

Notes ride four carved rune lanes down the longship deck toward the golden Hit Line near the stern. Strike D F J K as each note crosses the line.

2. Timing Judgments & Combo

CLEAN HIT: Full score, refills Crew Cohesion, and builds your Combo Multiplier (up to 5x).
WEAK HIT: Rows the crew for partial score.
MISS: Resets Combo and drains Cohesion.

3. Crew Cohesion & Sinking

The shared Crew Cohesion Meter refills on Clean Hits and empties on Misses. If Cohesion reaches 0%, the rowers lose time and the longship founders and sinks.

4. War Cry & Voyage Score

Filling Cohesion to 100% unleashes a WAR CRY—every Clean Hit earns 2x Bonus Score until a Miss ends it. Reach the Required Score before the song ends to win!

5. Hold Swells & Twin Oars

Later voyages add Twin Oar Chords (two lanes struck together) and Deep Swell Holds (hold the key down along the glowing wake tail until the end of the wave).

6. The Living Sea

Waves and weather rise with the song, pitching the ship and sweeping spray across the deck. Read the true rhythm through the storm motion—the sea never alters the musical beat.
