How to Play

Controls

  • W A S D or Arrows Move & steer across the snow
  • Mouse Turn the camera to look around
  • Space Jump over crevasses & spurs
  • Esc Pause / Resume or go back
  • Secret move taught on the first steep slope!

The Slide & Surfaces

On steep slopes, drop to your belly to build massive speed, though faster speed makes steering wider. Packed snow gives balanced grip, loose snow slows you down, and sheer blue ice preserves high momentum with lower grip.

Hazards, Shortcuts & Clock

Each mountain starts a countdown clock. Crevasses, ice spurs, and falling snow slabs knock the mother down and cost recovery time. Hunt hidden chutes & ice caves to cut distance!

Predator, Bosses & Power-Ups

The Skua circles overhead and swoops at you. On Boss mountains, dodge its talons and strike the resonant ice crystals/snowbanks to drive it off! Collect Fish for score, Speed-Bursts, and Ice Shields.
