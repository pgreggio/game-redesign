Keep your momentum

Downhill movement is automatic. Hold Shift: build speed. Fast slides need wider turns. Release to coast; hold ↓ (S) or Brake while steering for sharper turns. Packed trails hold speed. Deep snow slows you; ice makes steering slippery. Fish add a running-speed bonus of up to +10, not time. Collisions can cost momentum. Checkpoints top up time; fast arrivals keep their extra time.

Read the mountain

Tap ↑: jump over gaps and low rocks, or escape snow holes. W or Space also works. Release before jumping again. Fast ramp launches reach raised ice tunnels and clear deep snow. A cyan rim signals Power Slide: break white, cracked snow walls on contact. Solid rocks and ice spikes never break. Gold Boost adds speed for 7 seconds. Blue Shield protects against rocks, snow walls, ice spikes, and wolves for up to 5 seconds. Each protected hit uses 1.5 seconds; extra pickups do not extend it. Crevasses, falling rocks, and eagle attacks remain dangerous. Orange arrows mark jumps. Green marks wider routes; blue marks shorter, harder routes.

Outsmart the boss

Use left, right, and Jump. The orange target follows you. When it turns red and stops, dodge or jump! The first encounter ends after one dodge. Later, line up with a rock, wait for the target to lock, then dodge so the eagle hits it. Eagle Peak needs two rock hits. The timer stops after the last required rock hit. Your rescued chicks wait behind you.

Touch & menus

Hold Left / Right to steer, Slide to gain speed, and Brake to slow down. Tap the up button to jump; release before jumping again. Gamepad controls are listed above. Use arrow keys + Enter or D-pad + A in menus. Esc / P or Start toggles pause during gameplay. Completed rescues save automatically.
