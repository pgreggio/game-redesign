TACTICAL BRIEFING

HOW TO PLAY

MOVEMENT & AIMING W / A / S / D to move. Hold Shift to sprint between cover.
Mouse aims your weapon and turns your view. Left Mouse Button fires a single precision round.
THE JUDGEMENT SHOT & FAIL RULE Every shot travels in a straight line and hits the first thing it crosses. Nothing deflects an errant round. Harming any civilian fails the room immediately. Robbers' stray bullets can also strike civilians!
COVER, LEAN & RELOAD Press C or Left Ctrl behind desks, counters, or pillars to crouch in cover. Move or aim at cover edges (or press Q / E) to lean out and fire. Press R to reload (drops remaining rounds in the current magazine).
ARREST vs. NEUTRALIZE & RESCUE Press E near a robber who is flanked, reloading, disoriented, or surrendering to Arrest (Cuff) them alive and recover spare ammo. Press E near hostages to Rescue them, or on Vantage Doors with a Keycard.
